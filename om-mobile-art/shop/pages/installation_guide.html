Installation Guide

Follow these steps for a perfect bubble-free application.

What You Need

  • check_circleYour OM Mobile Art skin
  • check_circleMicrofiber cloth (included)
  • check_circleSqueegee or credit card
  • check_circleClean, dry workspace

Before You Start

  • Remove your phone case
  • Turn off the phone
  • Work in good lighting
  • Avoid dusty environments
1

Clean Your Device

Use the microfiber cloth to thoroughly clean the back and sides of your device. Remove all fingerprints, dust, and oils. A clean surface is crucial for proper adhesion.

2

Align the Skin

Without removing the backing, position the skin over your device to check alignment. Note the positions of camera cutouts, buttons, and edges.

3

Peel Corner Partially

Peel back about 1-2 cm of the backing from one corner. Align this corner with your device and press gently to anchor it.

4

Slowly Apply

With the anchored corner positioned, slowly peel the backing while smoothing the skin onto the device from center outward using the squeegee.

5

Smooth Out Bubbles

Use the squeegee to push any air bubbles toward the edges. Start from center and work outward. Small bubbles often disappear within 24 hours.

6

Final Press

Firmly press all edges and corners. Give special attention to curved edges. Your skin is now ready! Enjoy your premium look.
